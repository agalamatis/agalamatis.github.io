Wounds of War

A compendium of medieval combat injuries — 50 body-part & weapon-source combinations, each cataloguing five wound variants across three severities. Effects are expressed in GURPS Fourth Edition terms (attributes, skills, and traits) and grounded in the historical record of medieval battlefield medicine.

How to read this table. Each row is a body location struck by a specific weapon type; each column is a severity tier. Minor wounds heal on their own with time. Major wounds require sustained treatment (a Physician-skill roll and weeks of care under GURPS Low-Tech medical rules) to heal properly. Critical wounds threaten death within moments unless treated immediately. Click any cell to open its five wound entries, complete with description and instant / short-term / long-term game effects.
