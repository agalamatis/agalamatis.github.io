All 64 World Heritage properties in Italy, San Marino and the Vatican are tagged — 53 matched to existing Lonely Planet rows, 11 added as rows of their own — and 17 of the 30 tentative-list entries are represented, marked WHS-T. Italy has never had a property delisted, so there is no former-site category. Tags for large serial and urban properties are applied at destination level: every attraction inside the inscribed area carries the tag. Sources: Lonely Planet’s points-of-interest index for Italy, San Marino and Vatican City, the UNESCO World Heritage List and English Wikipedia. Founding dates come from Wikidata and from the opening paragraph of each Wikipedia article; rows with no article of their own are dated by site type, which is an estimate rather than a source. Where cultures overlap in time, the era is resolved by an explicit ethnonym in the name first, then by region. Map links are searches, not pins: Lonely Planet publishes no coordinates.
