All 56 of France’s inscribed World Heritage properties are tagged, and so is every one of the 31 entries on its tentative list (marked WHS-T). France has never had a property delisted, so there is no former-site category here. Lonely Planet, Wikipedia and map links come from the thirteen regional files; sixteen rows were added for World Heritage entries those files did not reach, including the six overseas properties. Founding dates come from Wikidata and from the opening paragraph of each Wikipedia article; rows with no article of their own are dated by site type, which is an estimate rather than a source.
