All 55 of Germany’s inscribed World Heritage properties are tagged, along with all 11 entries on its tentative list (marked WHS-T) and the Dresden Elbe Valley, inscribed in 2004 and struck off in 2009 over the Waldschlösschen bridge — the only German property ever delisted (marked WHS-X). Lonely Planet, Wikipedia and map links come from the twelve state files; five rows were added for World Heritage entries those files did not reach. Founding dates come from Wikidata and from the opening paragraph of each Wikipedia article; rows with no article of their own are dated by site type, which is an estimate rather than a source.
