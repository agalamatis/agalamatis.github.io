The ten eras, by number of entries

All 8 of Slovakia’s World Heritage properties are tagged and all 12 of its tentative-list entries are represented. Seven inscribed properties and five tentative entries were matched to Lonely Planet listings; the beech-forest inscription and seven tentative entries have no Lonely Planet listing and were added as rows of their own, marked “not in Lonely Planet” — Stužica, the Gemer and Abov wall-painting churches, the Muránska planina meadows, the Chatam Sófer memorial, the Komárno fortifications, the Bukovské fungal flora, the Herľany geyser and the church of St Margaret at Kopčany. Slovakia has never had a property delisted, so there is no former-site category. Three eras are empty — Pre-Historic, Lombard and Avar — which is a gap in Lonely Planet’s index rather than in Slovakia: the Moravany Palaeolithic sites, the Lombard cemeteries of the middle Danube and the Avar burials of the southwest are simply not listed. Slovakia’s eight kraje all appear; Lonely Planet’s own East / West / Central groupings cut across them, so the 21 sights filed that way were placed by naming the town each stands in. Founding dates come from Wikidata and from the opening paragraph of each Wikipedia article; rows with no article of their own — 74 of 121 — are dated by site type, which is an estimate rather than a source. A direct Wikipedia lookup on Slovak sight names returns many generic articles and foreign namesakes, so every candidate was checked against its own opening paragraph and eight false matches were dropped. The town inscriptions — Banská Štiavnica, Bardejov, Levoča with Spišský hrad — are tagged at destination level. Map links are searches, not pins: Lonely Planet publishes no coordinates.
